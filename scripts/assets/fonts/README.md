# Preview fonts

Noto Sans Regular and Bold are bundled for build-time article preview rendering.
They are not added to the frontend bundle or fetched during builds.

Downloaded from the [Noto Fonts project](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/tree/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans)
on October 18, 2026. The unmodified fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; see [OFL.txt](OFL.txt).
